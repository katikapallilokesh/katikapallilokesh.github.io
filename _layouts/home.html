About

I am a Predoctoral Researcher contributing to transparent and reliable vision-language models and multimodal foundation models for healthcare AI systems that can understand, reason, and interact using both visual and textual information. Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant in the Vision Language and Learning Group led by Prof. Anand Mishra at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, where I developed clinical AI systems in collaboration with Prof. Dr. Somesh Gupta from the All India Institute of Medical Sciences, addressing challenges in clinical decision-making arising from limited patient-reported symptoms and doctor-patient consultations.
